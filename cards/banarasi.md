# Banarasi Silk

- **Region:** Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh; GI districts: Varanasi, Jaunpur, Mirzapur, Chandauli, Bhadohi, Azamgarh
- **Era:**
  - documented: Pali literature mentions Banarasi fabric; first known for cotton, later famous for brocades (kimkhab) under the Mughal emperors and the Nawabs of Awadh
- **Technique:** woven on a pit loom, with small shuttles carrying silk and zari (gold and silver threads) for the brocade designs
- **Visual markers:**
  - fine silk with zari decoration; Mughal-inspired kalga and bel (floral and leaf) motifs
  - floating threads on the reverse of handloom pieces, not present in machine-made ones
  - heavy gold brocade pallu
- **GI tag:** 4 September 2009
