# Bandhani

- **Region:** Gujarat (called Bandhej), Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh (Bandhani); main centres Jamnagar and Kutch
- **Era:**
  - documented: according to the Ministry of Textiles, the Khatri community introduced Bandhani to Kutch in the 12th century
  - tradition: the claim of 5,000 years of continuous practice is an oversimplification, though tie-dyeing in India does have deep roots
- **Technique:** small portions of fabric are pinched and tied with thread, then dyed; the tied parts resist the dye and leave dots
- **Visual markers:**
  - patterns made of hundreds of tiny dots
  - slight irregularity in hand-tied dots; thread marks on the reverse
  - light colour gradient within each dot; machine prints show perfectly uniform dots
  - not to be confused with Leheriya, a separate Rajasthani tie-dye with wave-like lines
- **GI tag:** Jamnagari Bandhani has GI registration (year unclear in sources; confirm in the GI Registry)
