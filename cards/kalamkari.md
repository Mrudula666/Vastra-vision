# Srikalahasti Kalamkari

- **Region:** Srikalahasti, Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh
- **Era:**
  - documented: oldest surviving samples date from the 15th–16th centuries
  - tradition: likely older in Srikalahasti, but cloth decay and lack of written records mean it cannot be proven
- **Technique:** entirely hand-drawn with a kalam (pen); only natural dyes; about 23 steps
- **Visual markers:**
  - scenes and characters from the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Puranas; flower, animal and bird motifs
  - bold outlines, earthy colours
  - not to be confused with Machilipatnam (Pedana) Kalamkari, which is block-printed with Persian-style floral designs
- **GI tag:** 2006
