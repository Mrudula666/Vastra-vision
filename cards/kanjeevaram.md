# Kanjeevaram Silk

- **Region:** Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu
- **Era:**
  - documented: weaving records in Kanchipuram go back to the 10th century CE, linked to the Saliyar community from Andhra Pradesh; the modern form owes much to Rukmini Devi Arundale and Kalakshetra
- **Technique:** body, border and pallu woven separately and joined with the korvai technique
- **Visual markers:**
  - strong contrast colours, such as a mango-yellow body with a deep maroon border
  - temple border (reku) and mythical bird motifs
  - shot-colour band between the pallu and the body
  - heavy, broad gold zari border
- **GI tag:** 2 June 2005
