# Mangalagiri Cotton

- **Region:** Mangalagiri town, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh (home of the Panakala Narasimha Swamy temple)
- **Era:**
  - tradition: said to have been woven for over 500 years
  - documented (weak source, replace if a stronger one is found): the Nizam court of Hyderabad was a patron in the 18th–19th centuries
- **Technique:** handloom weaving of fine-count cotton; light and breathable
- **Visual markers:**
  - Nizam border of Surat zari with inverted checks and fine dots
  - plain or striped cotton body with a contrasting gold zari or silk border
  - imitation warning: printed Nizam borders are common fakes; the real border is woven with supplementary threads
- **GI tag:** 2010 (for the zari design), renewed in 2020
