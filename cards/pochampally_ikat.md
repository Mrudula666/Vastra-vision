# Pochampally Ikat

- **Region:** Bhoodan Pochampally, Yadadri–Bhuvanagiri district, Telangana; locally called Paagadu Bandhu or Chitki
- **Era:**
  - documented: weaving in Pochampally recognised from the 1950s; the wider Ikat tradition in India is much older (Gujarat, Odisha)
  - tradition: the technique is believed to have come from Chirala, where it is called chit-ku
- **Technique:** double ikat, where both warp and weft threads are resist-dyed before weaving so the pattern matches when woven
- **Visual markers:**
  - bold, abstract, geometric motifs
  - clear, precise patterns rather than the curvy or blurry patterns of some other Ikat styles
  - pattern visible on both sides of the fabric
- **GI tag:** 2005 (some sources say December 2003; confirm in the GI Registry)
